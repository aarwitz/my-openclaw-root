## 1. The hole Aaron found today (and the diagnosis that matters more than the hole)

Aaron predicted the system would be ignorant of the SaaS-sentiment reversal he told it about
weeks ago (NOW/CRM re-rating up, AAPL rewarded for staying out of AI capex). **Verified —
completely ignorant:** zero occurrences of "saas" in any table; NOW and CRM one hypothesis
each, ever; **AAPL has never been held once** while returning +21% in the last month
(NOW +12.5%, CRM +14.6% over the same window, from our own bar data). Most telling: the
system's own market_events recorded the *other half* of the rotation ("Tesla profit-quality
disappointment" 07-22, "mega-cap tech shifts from AI growth to capex" 07-23) and could not
connect the halves.

This is not a missed trade; it is five structural properties compounding:

1. **No object can hold a theme.** The world model's only learning unit is the single-name
   mechanism (generic antecedent→consequent with a Beta posterior). A cross-name narrative
   developing over weeks has no table, no lifecycle, no score. It cannot be learned because
   it cannot be stored.
2. **Operator knowledge has no landing pad.** What Aaron tells the system lands in chat
   sessions/agent memory files, which no trading pass reads.
3. **All research is bottom-up.** Every origination path starts from per-ticker screens.
   Nothing asks "which groups are inflecting and what story explains the cross-section?"
4. **The universe is screen-shaped.** AAPL never passed a screen, so the desk never even
   evaluated the largest stock in the market. Mega-cap sentiment turns are invisible until
   they become momentum — late by construction.
5. **Event lessons don't accumulate.** Daily debrief prose feeds nothing downstream
   (extract_patterns has been returning 0). Five consecutive lessons about the same force
   never become one standing belief.

**Fix filed as buildable structure** (not a one-off seeding): TM-293 (themes table +
lifecycle + operator CLI + debrief tagging, seeded with these three themes as the test),
TM-294 (deterministic industry-group relative-strength scanner — themes from prices alone;
acceptance requires it flags the software inflection on an as-of replay), TM-295 (forced
retrieval into research passes + market-graded theme scoreboard so wrong narratives die
visibly). None built yet.

## 2. Why we haven't beaten a coin flip / the S&P (honest decomposition)

Headline numbers: all-time +5.95% vs SPY +0.04% (**+5.91 alpha, banked in May–June**);
system-era (full autonomy since 07-07) +0.81% vs SPY −1.31% (**+2.12 alpha — real but mostly
defensive beta during a chop, not proven selection**); Brier 0.254 ≈ coin flip over 155
resolved predictions; realized selection alpha **−192 bps over 33 closed trades** (improving:
−37 bps last week vs −357 earlier).

The reasons, ranked by evidence:

1. **The judgment layer has measured *negative* skill so far.** The same inversion has now
   appeared in four independent measurements: p_correct anti-predictive live (corr −0.18,
   n=157), backtest LLM conviction anti-predictive at extremes, high-confidence hypotheses
   worst bucket (−294 bps vs medium −122), big-story direction 9/26 correct. The desk's
   *process* (gates, stops, drift mechanisms, cash yield) has been carrying its *judgment*.
2. **Horizon mismatch — now partially fixed.** The only validated edges are long-horizon
   (event_drift_up at 63d, FDR-proven; trend_1_3m the only positive realized bucket at
   +129 bps avg) yet 16/33 closed trades were sub-month, where the desk measurably loses.
   The horizon tilt (0.5× sub-month sizing, proven-mechanism gate on intraday/swing) went
   live TODAY — verified on this morning's fills. Its effect is unmeasured yet.
3. **Structural blindness to the month's dominant rotation** (section 1). The alpha that was
   available to a theme-aware allocator was never in the opportunity set. You cannot pick
   what you cannot see.
4. **The measurement substrate was broken until this week.** Resolver silently dead 3 days;
   stop enforcement dead a full session; partial pre-market bars leaking into point-in-time
   features (twice); a position unpriceable for 3 weeks after a ticker rename (BK→BNY) —
   including a fill priced off a stale close. Learning cannot compound on corrupted
   measurement. Integrity is now enforced (0 broken loops as of this writing) — but the
   *clean-measurement era is days old*. Most "learning" before it is suspect.
5. **One and a half regimes of data.** Every graded prediction comes from the 06-12
   momentum unwind plus its recovery. Calibration (Brier) can hardly beat 0.25 when the
   sample is one regime and probabilities were never validated to discriminate.
6. **Deliberate under-deployment.** Protect-first kept ~45% cash (now ~17% idle) and ~0.55
   beta into a rising tape — roughly half the SPY gap at its worst was this, by design,
   given Aaron's stakes. That is a choice, not a bug; revisit only with proven selection.
7. **A possible measurement unfairness worth checking:** judgment lines are graded at 10td,
   but the desk's theses increasingly live at 1–3 months. Grading a quarter-horizon call on
   a two-week clock may be systematically mis-scoring the judgment layer (see §4).

What has genuinely worked: the deterministic protection/process layer, cash yield, the drift
mechanisms, the walk-forward kill machine (options killed, WSB killed today with D79 —
IC 0.0045/t 0.40 on 344k submissions, no mercy), and the gates that kept the BSX thesis
(challenged, never sized, stock then −36%) from ever touching capital.

## 4. Other holes that plausibly exist (predicted, mostly unverified — check before trusting)

The pattern behind every hole found so far: *a capability everyone assumed existed, that
nothing measured.* Applying that pattern forward:

- **Grading-horizon mismatch (most likely real):** 10td grading vs 1–3m theses (§2.7).
  A deterministic re-grade of resolved judgment lines at horizon-matched windows would
  confirm or clear it in an afternoon.
- **Universe blindness beyond AAPL:** what else never enters screens? Mega-cap quality
  compounders, recent IPOs, ADRs. Nobody has diffed the tracked universe against the
  investable market by market cap. (TM-294's scanner partially addresses the group level;
  name-level gaps remain.)
- **Exit/falsifier quality:** stops and falsifiers fire, but the thresholds (−8% hard stop,
  falsifier rules) have never been backtested. The stop-postmortem stream exists to learn
  from this; nothing consumes it yet.
- **Portfolio-level correlation risk:** caps are per-name and gross. Factor concentration
  was measured once (53% pro-cyclical origination). No correlation/crowding cap is
  enforced anywhere.
- **Regime machinery untested outside neutral:** the classifier has been pinned at neutral
  (by design) its entire life. Desk behavior in a declared risk_on/risk_off regime has
  never executed once.
- **Macro calendar consumption:** macro_releases data exists; whether any pass actually
  de-risks/pre-positions around CPI/FOMC has not been verified.
- **Corporate-action coverage:** BK→BNY exposed renames; splits/dividends are handled in
  sim, but nothing reconciles the sim's CA stream against an external CA feed. The
  unmarkable-position tripwire catches the *symptom* (unpriceable), not all mispricings.
- **Post-PR uncommitted-edit pattern:** three times today a completed lane run left final
  edits (verdict docs) uncommitted after its PR. The sweeper can't see pre-PR/post-PR
  orphans. Until TM-292 lands, assume any "succeeded" lane run may have left debris —
  provenance-check finds it.
- **Single-token fleet auth:** every agent shares one Codex OAuth token; the host claude
  CLI is a second single point of failure (it silently killed the resolver once already).
  Expiry is now fail-loud, but there is no redundancy.
